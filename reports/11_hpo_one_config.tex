\textbf{The configuration.} Inside and outside edges get two separate colors (their own weights
in the GNN), trained with GLASS's recipe on GLASS's splits. With copies, every shared node is
replaced by one copy per subgraph.

\textbf{What we ran}, per dataset:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item An Optuna search over aggregation, pooling, hidden size, layers, lr, dropouts, batch
        size, weight decay, norm, jumping knowledge, head, and (synthetic only) edge counts.
        Each trial is 3 seeds, scored by mean validation. Trials: hpo\_neuro 80, hpo\_metab and
        em\_user 160, the rest 300. Weak trials were stopped after seed 0.
  \item The 3 best trials on 10 seeds. The one with the best 10-seed validation is reported.
        Test was never used to choose.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{The table} shows test micro-F1 (\%), mean $\pm$ std over 10 seeds; bold is the best in
each row.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{One config:} the search above. The real datasets ran without copies, because their
        copies graphs did not fit in GPU memory then. The synthetic datasets ran with copies.
  \item \emph{Copies, free coloring:} the real datasets searched again with copies. The search
        also picked the coloring: inside / outside, inside split by copy, or inside / outside plus
        clique. Attention was left out.
  \item \emph{Copies, one coloring:} inside / outside with copies everywhere. On ppi\_bp,
        hpo\_neuro and em\_user, it reuses the free-coloring winner's settings with only the
        coloring changed.
  \item \emph{Copies, more regularization:} the 3 free-coloring finalists of ppi\_bp and em\_user,
        each with more dropout (+0.2), more weight decay ($\times$10, at least $10^{-3}$), or both.
        The one with the best validation is reported.
  \item \emph{GLASS and GPEN:} published numbers from the GPEN paper.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Notes.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item em\_user colorings. Copies, free coloring: inside / outside plus the clique, with copies.
        Copies, one coloring: inside / outside with copies.
  \item Why ppi\_bp and em\_user trail GLASS and GPEN, two guesses:
  \begin{enumerate}
    \item Overfitting. Train F1 is about 99.8 on both em\_user and ppi\_bp, against 89 on
          hpo\_metab, where we beat the baselines.
    \item Noisy validation. Both have small validation sets (em\_user has about 65 subgraphs),
          so the search picks winners by luck. ppi\_bp's winner scores 69.6 on validation but
          58.6 on test, and on em\_user validation and test barely track each other (0.22
          correlation).
  \end{enumerate}
\end{itemize}
